Part 1 — Day Zero: Blank Slate to Verified EVPN/VXLAN Fabric

~5 sec
Open Network Ops → Workshop → Step 1 → click Clean Slate (Full Reset) → confirm.

Destroys any running ContainerLab containers, deletes generated configs & topology YAML, and wipes the 6 fabric devices, cables, IPs, and config contexts from NetBox. Click Back to Workshop — top counters now show 0/6, Missing, 0/6, 0/6.

Talk track: "Everything is empty — no devices in NetBox, no configs on disk, no running switches. We're building the entire 2-spine, 4-leaf EVPN/VXLAN fabric from NetBox without touching a CLI."

~20 sec
Step 3 → click Run Full Import → wait for green banner → Back to Workshop.

Populates Site DC1, 6 Arista cEOS devices, interfaces, IPs, 12 cables, custom fields (ASN, router-id, VTEP IP), tags, and 8 config contexts. Click Browse Devices or View Topology Map to show NetBox as the Source of Truth.

Talk track: "NetBox now holds the entire intent — no hardcoded values in playbooks or templates."

~2 sec
Step 4 → click Generate Topology → Back to Workshop → click Inspect Topology YAML.

Queries NetBox devices and cables and writes clab/topology-docker.clab.yml (6 nodes, 12 links).

~18 sec
Step 5 → click Generate Configs → Back to Workshop → click spine-01.cfg and leaf-01.cfg chips to inspect.

Ansible reads NetBox via dynamic inventory and renders all 6 EOS configs (BGP underlay, MLAG pair config, Vxlan1 VNI mappings, EVPN address family, tenant VLANs).

~60s deploy + ~60s boot
Step 6 → click Deploy Fabric → wait for green banner → allow ~60 seconds for Arista cEOS switches to finish booting.

Runs through the locked-down lab-runner sidecar — NetBox itself has no Docker socket access.

Talk track: "Notice the security boundary: NetBox never holds the Docker socket. It only calls fixed, allowlisted verbs on an isolated sidecar."

~5 sec
Step 7 → click Push Full Configs → verify all 6 switches show OK.

Loads each rendered config onto all 6 running cEOS containers and saves write memory. Wait ~20 seconds for BGP & MLAG to converge.

~3 sec
Step 8 → click Run Live Fabric Checks.

Shows 13 of 13 PASS cards live over eAPI: Spine BGP peers, all 4 Leaf MLAG Active statuses, Vxlan1/VNI table, EVPN BGP summary, Tenant VLANs & VRF CORE, and 4 cross-fabric loopback pings. Expand any card's Show CLI output to prove it's live.

~20 sec
Step 9 → click Run Full Validation.

Runs playbooks/test_all.yml across all 6 validation categories (Reachability, BGP, MLAG, Routing, Pings, VXLAN) and returns green.

Cleanup
Option A (show decommissioning): Click Retire Tenant VLAN / VNI → pick VLAN 160 (SHOWCASE_APP) → Preview → Approve & apply. Then click Detect & Remediate Config Drift to confirm compliance.
Option B (instant restore): On Workshop → Day 2 bar → click Rollback to Backup to restore flash:backup.cfg across all 6 switches.

Quick Recovery Reference

  • Switches still booting after Step 6 Deploy? Wait ~60 seconds, then click Push Full Configs again.
  • BGP/MLAG check fails right after Step 7 Push? Wait ~20 seconds for eBGP & MLAG to converge, then click Run Live Fabric Checks again.
  • Need to reset everything for the next audience? Open Workshop → Step 1 → Clean Slate (Full Reset).
